## Family-learning refinement — owner-approved November 6

These are requirements for ideas, full plans, revisions and substitutions, including revisions of previously generated plans:

- Every activity encourages natural parent–child interaction and working together. Parents participate in exploration, making, discussion or problem-solving, beyond preparation, supervision or grading. Do not force constant interaction or scripted bonding. Older children's independent portions can feed into a shared result or exchange. Honor actual adult availability and participant counts.
- Activities support primary teaching: children mainly rehearse, process and apply information already learned. Brief reminders and natural discoveries are welcome; a replacement teaching unit is not the default.
- Separate parent preparation normally totals no more than 10–15 minutes across the entire project, including a week-long activity. Quick retains its stricter five-minute setup limit. Count gathering, printing, reading ahead, research and material-making. State the total in the full plan's preparation section; prefer much less.
- Supply essential short reminders, role prompts, scenarios and examples directly in the existing preparation/session steps. Do not tell parents to assemble source packets, write summaries or design the activity themselves. Clearly label factual summaries, invented role prompts and hypothetical consequences; do not fabricate primary quotations or verified-source claims.
- Family research together can be appropriate at any age with suitable guidance. No conventional independent research assignment for a nine-year-old. Bounded independent research can fit around age 14 onward when skills fit, with findings contributing to the shared experience.
- Use profile interests only for a direct, useful connection. Necessary accessibility and safety accommodations remain required. Never assign the real thinking to one child and decoration or errands to another.
- A week-long choice need not become five formal lessons. Roles and session lengths must fit actual ages and group size. Keep supplies, directions and safety notes concise, specific and relevant.

The existing structured plan format is sufficient: ready-to-use short material belongs in preparation/session steps. No new model, UI flow, database storage or schema change is needed.

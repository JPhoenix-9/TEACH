# TEACH editorial standards

TEACH welcomes capable, curious parents at every stage of homeschooling. Lead with learning, family life, useful resources and participation.

Use messaging about anxiety, overwhelm, fear or panic sparingly and strategically, only when the specific topic or the reader's expressed situation warrants it. Do not assume every visitor is struggling, isolated or in distress. General room welcomes, navigation and recurring product prompts should sound sociable and inviting.

Approved Front Porch caption: “Pull up a chair. Join a conversation.”

Preserve approved product and webinar titles, genuine customer quotations, and original published articles. A stress-focused webinar or an article specifically discussing patience can address that subject directly; do not turn its language into the default tone across the website.

Describe availability accurately. Working accounts, profiles, My Desk, Library and purchaser community must not be described as unavailable. Generators remain Coming Soon. Checkout, missing recordings and existing-purchase matching must be described according to their actual release status.

Keep the locked homepage artwork and shared header unchanged. Approved artwork must stay consistent across pages. Describe backup and archive limits accurately; do not promise permanent hosting or guaranteed recovery.

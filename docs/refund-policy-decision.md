# GAP and Blueprint refund guarantee

Approved November 4, 2026: a 7-day money-back guarantee applies to all four GAP grade-band packages, all six individual Blueprint modules, and the complete Blueprint course. Customers may request a full refund within 7 days of purchase, including after downloading files or starting lessons. No explanation is required; feedback is optional.

Requests go through the existing private Contact & Help form in the purchasing family account. Ask for the product and purchase date, with receipt reference if available. There is no promised bank-processing or staff-response time. Earlier Homeschool-911 purchases keep their original terms. Later policy changes apply to future purchases; earlier purchases retain their offered terms.

Policy version: seven_day_v1. The guarantee is shown on both sales pages, on selection pages before payment, in the footer-linked /refunds policy, and in the Stripe Checkout submit-area text. Stripe session and payment-intent metadata record the policy version for each new checkout. No refund was issued and no automatic money-transfer/refund operation was added. Staff will process eligible requests through Stripe. Record reasons only when customers volunteer them to help evaluate policy performance.

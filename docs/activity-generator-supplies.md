# Activity Generator supplies and substitutions — November 5, 2026

Approved by Jana:
- Use a broad household baseline: clean empty plastic containers, toilet/paper-towel tubes, string/thread/yarn, simple paint, markers, pencils, tape, staples, glue, rulers, and basic tools such as screwdrivers and hammers.
- Parents should not have to invent an inventory before the generator can imagine activities.
- Every generated idea must include a supplies list before detailed instructions.
- Let parents identify missing items and ask for substitutions.
- Full plans also list supplies before preparation/instructions and offer substitutions; revisions must update supplies and instructions together.
- Substitutions must retain the learning goal, participants, time, whole-group budget, and permission-to-buy constraints. Unavailable items override baseline assumptions.
- Ordinary items may be assumed available or freely obtainable. A $0 budget never authorizes purchases.
- This replaces the mandatory up-front supplies checklist with idea-first supplies lists and on-demand substitutions.
- No price, included allowance, or $1.49 expansion-pack quantity change is implied.

# Backups and service closure

Owner decision October 22, 2026. This supersedes any wording implying that TEACH must operate its site or retain family archives forever. An ordinary subscription lapse still preserves reading and downloads while the archive service operates; closing the service is a separate event.

## Current terms and privacy
- Parents are responsible for current independent backup copies outside TEACH. Saved-to-account status does not mean an independent copy exists.
- Explain catastrophic internet, telecom, power, natural-disaster, hosting/database and cyber incidents outside reasonable control. Do not guarantee uninterrupted availability, loss prevention or recovery.
- Limit responsibility for data loss from events outside reasonable control and failure to keep independent backups, to the extent applicable law permits. Preserve nonwaivable rights and existing paid-purchase obligations; do not promise that a disclaimer defeats every claim.
- Reserve the right to change, suspend or discontinue the site, a tool or its archive. No perpetual operation or storage obligation.
- Implementation default: at least 30 calendar days after notice for retrieval during a planned permanent closure. The owner requested a defined retrieval period without specifying its length; the published default is 30 days and may be revised by the owner.
- Planned closure notice goes to affected account holders' account emails and appears on the website. It gives the actual date, retrieval deadline, affected tools and working export instructions. Keep retrieval available through the promised period; do not silently delete records during it.
- Catastrophic failures and urgent legal/security action may make advance notice, retrieval or recovery impossible. Notify as soon as reasonably practicable and accurately describe what remains available.
- After the deadline, no continuing retention/recovery obligation except legal requirements. Records may be removed through a separately authorized administrative process. This policy does not schedule deletion or close the service now.

## Future Transcript Generator and Legacy Generator
These tools remain Coming Soon and outside the initial build. Add these requirements when each is implemented; do not forget them during that separate project.

- Show a clear in-app backup reminder at a meaningful milestone, especially a completed transcript, completed legacy entry/chapter/project, or a substantial revision since the last backup reminder. Do not interrupt every keystroke or minor save.
- Proposed message: “You’ve created something important. It would be wise to download and retain a backup copy of this file for yourself. TEACH is not a permanent backup service.”
- Provide Download my copy and Remind me later actions. Use readable document exports (PDF and/or Word as appropriate), not raw-data dumps. Export the current complete project revision and include the TEACH copyright on printed/exported pages.
- Preserve the draft if export fails or the reminder is dismissed. A failed or cancelled download must not be represented as a successful backup.
- Keep save status separate from backup status. Download initiation does not prove the file was retained on the user's device; if a backup confirmation is needed, ask the parent explicitly whether they kept the copy.
- Track meaningful project versions/reminder acknowledgement per account and project; repeat after substantial later work. Exact milestone thresholds belong to each future generator's workflow and must be defined and tested then.
- Backups must not depend solely on an account remaining active, a browser cache or the TEACH service remaining online.

## Operational closure checklist
When a real planned closure is authorized: inventory affected files and legal retention duties; verify every export; set an explicit deadline at least 30 calendar days after notice; deliver the account notice separately from marketing consent; post a site banner; retain retrieval access through the deadline; stop selling incompatible access; resolve paid obligations; then conduct a separately authorized retention/deletion process. The current site does not contain an automated closure-notification or bulk-deletion system.
